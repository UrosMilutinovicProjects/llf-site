Common Questions

Before You Speak With Insurance

Should I give a recorded statement?

It is usually best to speak with an attorney first so you understand how the statement may affect your claim.

What documents should I keep?

Keep medical records, bills, photos, repair estimates, police reports, witness information, and all insurance communication.

What if the insurance company already called?

You can pause the conversation and request time to get legal guidance before answering questions or accepting an offer.

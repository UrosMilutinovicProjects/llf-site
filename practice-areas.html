Premises Liability

Personal Injury FAQs

How much does it cost to speak with LeUnes Law Firm?

The consultation is free. For personal injury matters, there are no upfront legal fees.

Do I deal directly with an attorney?

Yes. One of the firm's key differences is direct attorney involvement throughout the case.

What should I do after an accident?

Get medical attention, document what happened, avoid giving recorded statements without legal guidance, and speak with an attorney as soon as possible.

Does LeUnes Law Firm handle cases outside Houston?

Yes. Christopher LeUnes is licensed to help injured clients throughout Texas.

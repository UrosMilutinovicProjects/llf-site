Meet The Attorney

About Christopher C. (Chay) LeUnes

Christopher (Chay) LeUnes has practiced law in Texas since 1993. He founded the LeUnes Law Firm after a successful career as a defense attorney for a top Houston law firm, a Fortune 500 company, and the Office of the Attorney General. Chay focuses his practice on representing people injured in all types of motor vehicle accidents, including cars, motorcycles, and commercial trucks.

The LeUnes Law Firm lives by the principle that every client deserves individualized attention and a commitment to maximizing case results.

Chay graduated from Texas A&M University in 1989 and from St. Mary's University School of Law in 1993. Working as a defense lawyer for over 20 years, Chay learned the other side of the case in great detail, and he uses that experience to the benefit of his clients.

Practicing in Texas since 1993, Chay and the LeUnes Law Firm have helped thousands of clients get the results they deserve.

Questions About Working With Christopher

How long has Christopher practiced?

Chay has been licensed in Texas since 1993, giving him more than 30 years of legal experience. He spent over 20 of those years as a defense attorney before founding the LeUnes Law Firm.

Will I work directly with him?

Yes. Clients choose LeUnes Law Firm because they want direct access, clear communication, and personal attention.

Do consultations cost anything?

Personal injury consultations are free, and there is no fee unless we win. Real estate and estate planning matters are handled on a different fee basis, so please call to discuss those.

What types of cases does the firm focus on?

Chay focuses on motor vehicle accident cases involving cars, motorcycles, and commercial trucks, along with other serious personal injury and wrongful death matters. The firm also assists clients with real estate and estate planning.
